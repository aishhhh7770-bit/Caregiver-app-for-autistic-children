a complete therapeutic tablet app for autistic children with caregiver controls. The app features five main screens: Home with personalized greeting, 5-option emotion check-in (Joyful→Too Much) that triggers context-aware calming activities from an emotion-aware avatar, and checkable morning routines with AR launch buttons; Routines with three expandable daily schedules (Morning/After School/Bedtime), live progress tracking, and therapist sync status; Stories with categorized social stories narrated in caregiver voices, full scene-by-scene playback with dot navigation, and recording indicators; Progress tracking weekly completion charts, skill development bars, earned/locked achievement badges, and therapist notes; Caregiver Settings with adaptive sensory sliders (sound/visual intensity + quick presets), interactive home sensory map (tap rooms to cycle Calm/Moderate/Active zones), therapist collaboration controls, and privacy/safety toggles.
